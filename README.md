# Intentional Questions: Harnessing AI to ask better, live better and do better
In a world spinning faster by the day, where information overload drowns curiosity, Intentional Questions invites you to pause. To reflect. To ask better questions- of yourself, of your technology, and of your life. This isn’t just another book about AI. It’s a story, a journey, and a breathing experience built for everyday explorers- whether you're 18 or 80.

You don’t need to be an expert. You only need your innate curiosity. Inside, you’ll walk through seven mystical realms. You’ll gather Tokens. You’ll learn to use AI as a companion to think deeper, live slower, and create wiser.

What to expect:

1. Real-world AI tools explained in human language.

2. Practical prompts you can immediately apply to elevate your life.

3. Self-reflection checkpoints to grow your awareness.
